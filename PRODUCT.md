# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Enterprise buyers (confirmed): CTOs, heads of data, operations and digital at companies in pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, financial services, energy, healthcare, retail, logistics, telecoms and automotive, evaluating an AI and software engineering partner. They scan for credibility, fit with their industry, and a clear way to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

ardbergai is an AI and software engineering company based in Hyderabad, India. It turns an organisation's fragmented data, models and systems into one connected, working capability. Success for the site: a qualified buyer understands what ardbergai does, sees their industry reflected, and starts a conversation (mailto contact).

## Positioning

Started in life sciences, where AI has to be right, and brings that same rigour to every industry (from site copy). Mechanism, in its own words: "From cluster to clarity": scattered inputs (spreadsheets, legacy ERP, siloed data, PDF archives, disconnected tools) connected into one intelligence.

## Operating Context

Three ways to engage: building purpose-built platforms; partnering with client teams from first conversation to production; embedding engineers in client teams. Nine industries are listed with industry-specific challenges and signals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js 16 (App Router) + Tailwind v4 codebase; all copy lives in `lib/version2-content.ts`.
- This repository is a fork used for UI/UX redesign explorations of a friend's company site. Copy, information, sections, navigation and routes must be preserved; only presentation changes (confirmed).
- Several copy blocks are marked TODO placeholder in the source; they are still shipped as-is, never rewritten by design work.
- `/palette/*` preview routes exist and must keep working; a redesign may give them its own single look (confirmed for the ASCII exploration).

## Brand Commitments

- Name is written lowercase: "ardbergai". Logo mark at `public/logo.png`; wordmark is text.
- Site title line: "Where intelligence becomes impact".

## Evidence on Hand

Only the site copy. No case studies, client names, logos, testimonials, metrics or certifications exist in the repo: none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility first: the visitor must be able to scan what ardbergai does within seconds, however bold the presentation.
2. Show the mechanism (complexity becoming connected intelligence) rather than claim it.
3. Every industry should recognise itself.
4. One clear next step: start a conversation.
